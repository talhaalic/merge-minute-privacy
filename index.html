Privacy Policy

Merge Minute: Metro Puzzle · CraftByte Labs
Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Merge Minute: Metro Puzzle ("the Game"), published by CraftByte Labs ("we", "us"), handles information.

Summary

The Game does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it works fully offline.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal or device data. The Game does not require an account and does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (such as levels, scores, coins, unlocked items and settings) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes it permanently.

Advertising and in-app purchases

The Game does not show advertisements and does not offer real-money purchases. All in-game items are unlocked with virtual coins earned by playing.

Third-party services

The Game does not include any third-party SDKs that collect data. The Game is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Because the Game does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the Game's data practices change in a future update, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
